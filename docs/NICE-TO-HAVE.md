# Nice-to-have backlog

These ideas are intentionally outside the MVP and are not roadmap commitments. Each requires a new design decision before implementation.

## Operations

- Scheduled backups, retention policies, integrity schedules, and configurable backup destinations
- Multi-instance or multi-Household operation
- Managed hosting or automated public ingress

## Care and collaboration

- Restricted, read-only, babysitter, childcare, or time-bounded roles
- Household-defined custom care-record types
- Medication schedules, stock, reminders, and notifications
- Vaccinations and milestones
- Native daily diary writing
- Diary photos and general attachments
- Full-text note search and saved filters

## Clients and integrations

- Native iOS or Android applications
- Widgets, watches, voice assistants, and smart-speaker integrations
- Push notifications
- External health platforms, calendars, or medical systems

## Reporting and migration

- PDF and print-ready reports
- Additional PiyoLog export locales
- PiyoLog PDF or photo archival
- Importers for other baby-tracking applications
- Developmental percentiles or reference curves; any such feature requires separate medical and regulatory review

## Presentation

- Dutch, Japanese, and other Minilog interface translations
- Themes other than the dark-first baseline
